# Month 1: Foundation (IELTS Academic)

Goal: Build your base with diagnostic testing, grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, and understanding the exam format.

Suggested daily study time: 1.5–2 hours, 6 days a week.

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: Core skills + grammar
- Tuesday: Reading + vocabulary
- Wednesday: Listening + language review
- Thursday: Grammar + reading
- Friday: Listening + vocabulary + exam format
- Saturday: Practice + review
- Sunday: Rest or light review

---

## Day 1 – Diagnostic test + plan setup
- [ ] Take a full IELTS practice test or a full Cambridge Listening/Reading set.
- [ ] Mark your score honestly for Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.
- [ ] Identify your weakest skill.
- [ ] Set up a vocabulary notebook.
- [ ] Write down your goals for the next 30 days.
- [ ] Review your test results and note 3 main problems.

## Day 2 – Grammar foundation: tenses
- [ ] Review present, past, and future tenses.
- [ ] Do 15–20 grammar exercises on tense use.
- [ ] Write 5 sentences using each major tense.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words with example sentences.
- [ ] Listen to 15–20 minutes of English audio.
- [ ] Read one short article and underline useful vocabulary.

## Day 3 – Articles, prepositions, and sentence structure
- [ ] Review articles (a, an, the, zero article).
- [ ] Review prepositions of time, place, and movement.
- [ ] Practice sentence structure: simple, compound, complex sentences.
- [ ] Write 10 sentences using correct articles and prepositions.
- [ ] Learn 5–10 new words with definitions and example sentences.
- [ ] Read one article and summarize it in 4–5 lines.

## Day 4 – Reading basics + vocabulary
- [ ] Read one short IELTS-style article or newspaper piece.
- [ ] Identify main idea, supporting details, and difficult vocabulary.
- [ ] Practice 1 short reading set (or 1 passage, untimed).
- [ ] Write 5 new words in your notebook with simple definitions.
- [ ] Listen to one English podcast or BBC clip for 15–20 minutes.
- [ ] Note 3 words you heard and want to remember.

## Day 5 – Listening basics + exam format
- [ ] Review the format of all four IELTS sections.
- [ ] Learn the question types in Listening and Reading.
- [ ] Complete 1 Listening section (or 1 set of short listening tasks).
- [ ] Check spelling carefully after each answer.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words and review yesterday’s words.
- [ ] Write 3 mistakes you made and how to fix them.

## Day 6 – Writing habit + grammar review
- [ ] Review grammar basics: subject-verb agreement, punctuation, conjunctions.
- [ ] Write one short paragraph on a general topic.
- [ ] Focus on sentence variety and grammar accuracy.
- [ ] Read one article and note 5 useful phrases.
- [ ] Listen to English for 15–20 minutes.
- [ ] Review all vocabulary learned this week.

## Day 7 – Rest or light review
- [ ] Rest your brain.
- [ ] Do a light review of vocabulary and common grammar mistakes.
- [ ] Listen to one easy English audio clip.
- [ ] Avoid heavy practice; keep it light and calm.

## Day 8 – Reading and question types
- [ ] Study Reading question types: multiple choice, matching headings, sentence completion, T/F/NG.
- [ ] Complete one short reading passage untimed.
- [ ] Practice identifying keywords and scanning for answers.
- [ ] Write 5 new vocabulary items with example sentences.
- [ ] Listen to 15–20 minutes of spoken English.
- [ ] Review grammar from the previous week.

## Day 9 – Listening skills and note-taking
- [ ] Do one Listening practice section.
- [ ] Focus on note-taking and spelling accuracy.
- [ ] Review common listening traps: distractors and synonyms.
- [ ] Read one article and write a 5-sentence summary.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Note 3 mistakes from the listening section.

## Day 10 – Grammar: articles and prepositions
- [ ] Practice article rules again with short exercises.
- [ ] Review prepositions of time, place, movement, and cause.
- [ ] Write 10 correct sentences using difficult prepositions.
- [ ] Read one article and highlight words you want to use.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Listen to English for 15 minutes.

## Day 11 – Reading speed and comprehension
- [ ] Practice one reading passage with a timer (start untimed if needed).
- [ ] Focus on skimming and scanning.
- [ ] Write down the question type you struggled with most.
- [ ] Review those question types again.
- [ ] Learn 5–10 new words.
- [ ] Listen to a short speech or podcast.

## Day 12 – Listening: section patterns
- [ ] Review Listening section formats and common patterns.
- [ ] Do one full Listening set or 2 shorter sections.
- [ ] Check spelling and listen carefully for numbers, names, and dates.
- [ ] Read one article and record 5 new words.
- [ ] Write one paragraph about a topic you know well.
- [ ] Review grammar and vocabulary from this week.

## Day 13 – Writing paragraph + vocabulary
- [ ] Write one short paragraph (80–120 words) on a familiar topic.
- [ ] Focus on linking words and correct verb tenses.
- [ ] Review sentence connectors: however, therefore, in addition, moreover.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words with example sentences.
- [ ] Read one article and note useful phrases.
- [ ] Listen to English for 20 minutes.

## Day 14 – Review and light test practice
- [ ] Review all grammar topics studied so far.
- [ ] Do one short practice session for Reading or Listening.
- [ ] Check your error log for repeated mistakes.
- [ ] Update your vocabulary notebook.
- [ ] Write 3 sentences correcting common grammar errors.
- [ ] Take a break or do only light revision.

## Day 15 – Speaking basics: fluency and pronunciation
- [ ] Practice speaking for 10 minutes on a familiar topic.
- [ ] Focus on fluency, not perfection.
- [ ] Record yourself speaking for 1–2 minutes.
- [ ] Listen back and identify filler words or pauses.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Read one article and write a short summary.

## Day 16 – Reading + grammar refresh
- [ ] Complete one reading passage and identify difficult vocabulary.
- [ ] Review basic grammar rules again: sentence structure, agreement, punctuation.
- [ ] Write 5–10 sentences using correct grammar.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words and review last week’s words.
- [ ] Listen to English audio for 15–20 minutes.
- [ ] Do a brief recap in your notebook.

## Day 17 – Listening practice and spelling
- [ ] Do a Listening section and check all answers carefully.
- [ ] Focus on spelling, plurals, and numbers.
- [ ] Review tricky word forms (noun/verb/adjective).
- [ ] Read one article and highlight useful phrases.
- [ ] Learn 5 new vocabulary words.
- [ ] Write down any recurring listening errors.

## Day 18 – Reading strategies + summary writing
- [ ] Practice reading skills: skimming, scanning, and keyword matching.
- [ ] Complete one short passage and answer questions.
- [ ] Write a 4–5 sentence summary of the article you read.
- [ ] Review 5 new words.
- [ ] Listen to one English podcast or talk.
- [ ] Check your grammar notebook for weak areas.

## Day 19 – Grammar focus: conditionals and connectors
- [ ] Review conditionals (zero, first, second).
- [ ] Practice linking words and sentence connectors.
- [ ] Write 8–10 sentences using conditionals and connectors.
- [ ] Read one article and underline advanced words.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Listen to English for 15 minutes.

## Day 20 – Listening + speaking combination
- [ ] Do one Listening section.
- [ ] Speak for 10 minutes on a topic related to the listening content.
- [ ] Record yourself and note improvements needed.
- [ ] Review your vocabulary notebook.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words and use them in sentences.
- [ ] Read one article and summarize it quickly.

## Day 21 – Reading + exam format review
- [ ] Review IELTS Reading format and timing.
- [ ] Practice one passage under timed conditions if possible.
- [ ] Mark mistakes and note why you missed them.
- [ ] Review grammar basics that still feel weak.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Listen to English audio for 20 minutes.

## Day 22 – Listening section drill
- [ ] Do 1 full Listening section or 2 shorter tasks.
- [ ] Focus on understanding the question before listening.
- [ ] Practice with names, numbers, dates, and spelling-heavy answers.
- [ ] Read one article and note 5 useful phrases.
- [ ] Learn 5 new vocabulary items.
- [ ] Update your error log.

## Day 23 – Writing paragraph + grammar check
- [ ] Write one paragraph about a general topic or daily life.
- [ ] Check grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, and linking words.
- [ ] Review sentence structure and run-on sentences.
- [ ] Read one article and identify 5 useful words.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Listen to English audio for 15 minutes.

## Day 24 – Reading and vocabulary review
- [ ] Complete one reading passage and review your answers.
- [ ] Identify which question types are still difficult.
- [ ] Review the vocabulary from previous days.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words and write example sentences.
- [ ] Listen to one spoken text and practice note-taking.
- [ ] Write a short reflection: What improved this month?

## Day 25 – Listening and note-taking practice
- [ ] Do one Listening section with careful note-taking.
- [ ] Focus on understanding the main idea.
- [ ] Review your weak spelling patterns.
- [ ] Read one article and write a 5-sentence summary.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Review your error log.

## Day 26 – Grammar and sentence-building block
- [ ] Review common grammar issues: articles, prepositions, tenses, agreement.
- [ ] Write 10 sentences using correct grammar.
- [ ] Practice combining ideas with conjunctions.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words with example sentences.
- [ ] Read one article and underline useful vocabulary.
- [ ] Listen to English for 15–20 minutes.

## Day 27 – Reading + speaking practice
- [ ] Do one reading passage and review it carefully.
- [ ] Speak for 10 minutes on a familiar topic.
- [ ] Record yourself and listen back.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Read one article and make short notes.
- [ ] Note your speaking weak points (pauses, repetition, vocabulary gaps).

## Day 28 – Full mini review
- [ ] Review Listening, Reading, grammar, and vocabulary from the whole month.
- [ ] Do a short timed practice set if you are ready.
- [ ] Check which skills still need extra work next month.
- [ ] Update your vocabulary notebook.
- [ ] Write one paragraph and review it thoroughly.
- [ ] Listen to English audio for a final warm-up.

## Day 29 – Consolidation day
- [ ] Revisit your weakest skill from the month.
- [ ] Practice 30–45 minutes on that skill.
- [ ] Review one reading passage and one listening task.
- [ ] Learn 5 new words.
- [ ] Write a short reflection about your progress.
- [ ] Listen to English and note useful phrases.

## Day 30 – End-of-month assessment
- [ ] Take a short full practice test or a mixed set of Reading + Listening.
- [ ] Measure your improvement against Day 1.
- [ ] Review your biggest mistakes.
- [ ] Update your vocabulary notebook one last time.
- [ ] Write down your 3 main goals for Month 2.
- [ ] Celebrate the progress you made this month.

---

## Month 1 checkpoint
- [ ] I completed a diagnostic test and identified my weakest skill.
- [ ] I reviewed grammar basics and practiced regularly.
- [ ] I built a vocabulary notebook with daily words.
- [ ] I listened to English every day or most days.
- [ ] I read one article or short passage daily.
- [ ] I learned the exam format and question types.
- [ ] I reviewed my mistakes and kept a record of recurring errors.

Next step: Move into Month 2 with a stronger base in Reading and Listening.
