# IELTS Academic: 6-Month Study Plan

This plan assumes about 1.5 to 2 hours of study a day, six days a week.

## Month 1: Foundation

- Take a full practice test (Cambridge IELTS) to find your starting band and your weakest skill.
- Review grammar basics: tenses, articles, prepositions, sentence structure.
- Start a vocabulary notebook (5-10 new words a day with example sentences).
- Listen to English daily (BBC, TED, podcasts) and read one article a day.
- Learn the format and question types for all four sections.

## Month 2: Reading and Listening

- Practice one Reading passage a day, untimed at first, then timed (20 minutes).
- Learn each question type: True/False/Not Given, matching headings, sentence completion.
- Do Listening sections 1-4 with Cambridge audio, then check your spelling and the mistakes you missed.
- Write one short paragraph every day to build the writing habit.

## Month 3: Writing

- **Task 1:** Learn to describe line graphs, bar charts, tables, pie charts, and processes. Write 2-3 a week.
- **Task 2:** Learn the essay types (opinion, discussion, problem-solution, two-part questions). Write 2 essays a week.
- Get feedback from a teacher, tutor, or a writing correction tool. This matters most this month.
- Speak for 10 minutes a day, recording yourself.

## Month 4: Speaking and Weak Areas

- Practice all three Speaking parts with common topics (home, work, study, technology, environment).
- Practice with a partner or tutor if possible, and review your recordings for fluency, pauses, and pronunciation.
- Spend extra time on whichever skill your diagnostic showed as weakest.
- Do one timed Reading section and one Listening section every week.

## Month 5: Timed Practice

- Take one full mock test every week under real conditions (Listening, Reading, and Writing back to back, then Speaking on another day).
- Record your scores and analyze every mistake. Keep an error log so you stop repeating the same ones.
- Rewrite your weakest essays and improve them.
- Do 2-3 mock Speaking sessions with feedback.

## Month 6: Polish and Test

- Take 1-2 full mocks a week for the first three weeks.
- Revise your vocabulary notebook and your error log.
- Focus on time management and exam-day strategy.
- **Final week:** Light revision only. Sleep well, prepare your ID/passport, and know your test center location.

## Weekly Routine

| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Reading |
| Tue | Listening |
| Wed | Writing (Task 2) |
| Thu | Speaking + vocabulary |
| Fri | Writing (Task 1) |
| Sat | Full or partial mock test |
| Sun | Rest or light review |

## Tips

- Book your test date now, around month 5 or 6, so you have a fixed goal.
- Writing and Speaking need outside feedback to improve, so find a teacher or tutor if you can.
- Cambridge IELTS 14-19 are the best practice books.
